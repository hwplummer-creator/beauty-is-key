FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers before your visit. Still have a question? Call or text (703) 331-3092.

How do I book an appointment?

Appointments are by request. Send the style, size and length you want, and we'll confirm a time. A $25 deposit on Venmo (@Badkey127) holds your spot.

How long will my appointment take?

Plan on 3 hours or more for all braid appointments, 2 hours or more for cornrows and crochet, and 1½ hours or more for kids. Smaller and longer styles take longer. Your confirmation will include an estimate.

Do you provide the braiding hair?

Tell us when you book whether you're bringing your own or want us to supply it. Crochet prices don't include the cost of hair, and human hair for boho braids costs extra.

How should I prepare my hair?

Arrive with clean, fully detangled hair, ideally blow-dried or stretched. Skip heavy oils and gels, since they make it harder to braid.

How long do braids last?

Most last 4 to 8 weeks with good care. Wrap your hair at night, moisturize your scalp, and avoid pulling the style tight.

How often should I retwist my locs?

Most people retwist every 4 to 8 weeks. Your stylist can suggest a schedule based on how fast your hair grows.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, kids' braids (singles) start at $165, and kids' appointments run about 1½ hours or more. Please book their time separately and let us know ahead so we can plan.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give as much notice as you can. Deposits are non-refundable, but we'll work with you to move your appointment when possible.
