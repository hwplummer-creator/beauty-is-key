FAQ

Good to know

Sample answers for the salon to review and correct before the site goes live.

How do I book an appointment?

Appointments are by request. Send the style, size and length you want, and we'll confirm a time. A deposit holds your spot.

How long will my appointment take?

It depends on the style. Smaller and longer braids take more time, so a full set can run 4 to 7 hours. Loc retwists are usually 1 to 2 hours. Your confirmation will include an estimate.

Do you provide the braiding hair?

Tell us when you book whether you're bringing your own or want us to supply it. Supplied hair may add to the price.

How should I prepare my hair?

Arrive with clean, fully detangled hair, ideally blow-dried or stretched. Skip heavy oils and gels, since they make it harder to braid.

How long do braids and twists last?

Most last 4 to 8 weeks with good care. Wrap your hair at night, moisturize your scalp, and avoid pulling the style tight.

How often should I retwist my locs?

Most people retwist every 4 to 8 weeks. Your stylist can suggest a schedule based on how fast your hair grows.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, kids' braids are available for ages 12 and under. Please book their time separately and let us know ahead so we can plan.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give as much notice as you can. Deposits are non-refundable, but we'll work with you to move your appointment when possible.
